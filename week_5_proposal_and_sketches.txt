1. DATASET CHOICE
=================
Dataset:
  TMDB 5000 Movies Dataset
  Source: Kaggle via The Movie Database (TMDB) API
  URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tmdb/tmdb-movie-metadata

Rationale:
  Film economics represents a compelling visual domain because massive capital
  investments do not guarantee box office profitability or critical praise.
  Spanning 4,803 films from 1916 to 2017, this dataset enables exploring how
  budgets correlate with revenue, critical reception, popularity, and historical
  shifts across cinematic genres.

Dimensions (Mixing Quantitative, Ordinal/Temporal, and Categorical):
  * Production Budget (budget_millions): Quantitative (Continuous, $0M to $380M)
    Production expenditure in millions USD.
  * Box Office Revenue (revenue_millions): Quantitative (Continuous, $0M to $2,788M)
    Worldwide gross box office revenue in millions USD.
  * Popularity Score (popularity): Quantitative (Continuous)
    TMDB algorithmic user engagement metric.
  * Audience Vote Average (vote_average): Quantitative (Continuous Interval, 0.0 to 10.0)
    Crowdsourced user rating average.
  * Release Year (release_year): Temporal / Ordinal (1916 to 2017)
    Year of theatrical release, establishing chronological order.
  * Primary Genre (primary_genre): Categorical / Nominal
    Top 6 primary genres (Adventure, Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Crime) and "Other".
  * Movie Count (movie_count): Quantitative (Discrete)
    Frequency count of movies within rating distribution intervals.


2. THREE RESEARCH QUESTIONS
===========================
RQ1: Financial Scale, Return, and Critical Acclaim
  How does a movie's production budget correlate with its worldwide box office
  revenue, and do higher-budget blockbuster productions reliably achieve
  superior audience ratings and popularity?

RQ2: Genre Profitability vs. Audience Sentiment
  Which primary movie genres produce the highest average box office return,
  and does high financial return align with favorable audience ratings?

RQ3: Cinematic Era Evolution and Rating Distributions
  How has average film revenue evolved across cinematic eras (from early
  cinema to the modern blockbuster era), and do release eras exhibit distinct
  audience rating profiles?
